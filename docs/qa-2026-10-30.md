# Claude Native QA: October 30, 2026

## Composer controls in narrow columns

The real composer requested 723 points of width at standard text size and 1,600 points at 300 percent when it contained a long folder name, mode, model, Stop and Send. A hosted regression reproduced this at both 420- and 720-point column widths.

The controls now wrap in reading order. Model and sending controls align to the trailing edge of their row. Native menu buttons accept the width available to them and truncate their visible labels while retaining full accessibility names and their menu carets.

Rendered previews caught a second defect after the initial wrapping change: the native hosting view still drew a long folder label beyond its button at 300 percent. A regression now measures that inner view as well. The label uses the button's bounds instead of constraints that kept it wider than its button.

The new checks cover all six controls, their bounds and overlap, at both widths and text sizes. Eight native renders cover light and dark appearances. Inspected previews include both narrow appearances at 300 percent and the wider column at standard and 300 percent. Local images are in `build/qa-oct30/`.

## Short windows

The running QA app at 300 percent exposed Send below the bottom of its short window. A regression with the starting suggestions and composer reproduced a minimum height of 558 points for a 360-point host.

The suggestions now sit in a named scroll area and stay centered when they fit. The bar also reports a range of heights during SwiftUI's minimum- and maximum-size measurements. Treating its one-row height as a maximum had constrained the hosting view even after the controls wrapped. The final short-window and control-boundary tests pass, and a native screenshot confirms the entire Send control and card edge remain visible at 300 percent.

## Live VoiceOver

The scripting cursor followed the QA app correctly during this pass. At 300 percent, forward navigation reached the named message field, Add, Mode, Folder, Model and the dimmed Send button, in that order. The model menu opened through VoiceOver, reported six items with Offline QA checked, and returned to a collapsed button after Escape. The suggestions scroll area reported four items; interacting reached its heading and all three suggestion buttons.

Moving backward from the empty message field then found a separate blank stop. Interacting with it reported “In content is empty”. The app's live accessibility tree contained an `AXUnknown` element before the field. Typing into the field removed that element, identifying the decorative Canvas placeholder as the source despite its `accessibilityHidden` modifier.

The placeholder now draws through a native view that explicitly exposes neither an accessibility element nor children. In the rebuilt app with an empty field, `AXUnknown` is absent. VoiceOver moves directly backward from the field to “Session suggestions scroll area”, and forward from that area to the field and Add. Its visible text remains present. Offscreen hosted inspection did not reproduce the Canvas's blank stop; the before/after evidence for this fix is the live app and VoiceOver traversal.

Command-0 restored the single-row layout and normal placeholder size. VoiceOver's reported phrase included “Text size 100 percent”. The QA app was closed, its temporary text-size preference removed, and Claude desktop restored to the foreground. No message was sent.

VoiceOver evidence is reported text, not captured audio. Live checks covered the idle composer's five controls; Stop and the long folder name were checked in hosted geometry and renders. The wider release checklist remains applicable. Local evidence is in `build/qa-oct30/voiceover.jsonl` and `size-reset-watch.json`.
